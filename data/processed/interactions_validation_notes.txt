 # Validation Notes – Ramipril (M030)

Source PDF: RAMIPRIL ALMUS 1,25 mg
Source page: 3

## Section 4.5 – Interactions

- Sacubitril/valsartan: Contraindicated.
- Dialysis with negatively charged high-permeability membranes: Contraindicated.
- Potassium-sparing diuretics: Use with caution.
- Potassium supplements: Use with caution.
- Heparin: Use with caution.
- NSAIDs: Use with caution.
- Lithium: Use with caution.
- Antidiabetic drugs: Use with caution; risk of hypoglycemia.
- mTOR/NEP inhibitors: Use with caution.

## Section 4.8 – Adverse Effects

- Frequent: Hyperkalemia, headache, dizziness, hypotension, syncope, irritative cough, bronchitis, dyspnea, digestive disorders, nausea, diarrhea.
- Uncommon: Anorexia, loss of appetite, vertigo, paresthesia, dysgeusia, flushing, bronchospasm, nasal congestion, pancreatitis, intestinal angioedema.
- Rare: Tremor, balance disorder, vasculitis, glossitis.
- Very rare / Unknown: Hyponatremia, cerebral ischemia, stroke, Raynaud syndrome, acute liver failure.

Frequency column in the source combines "Very rare / Unknown"; exact frequency cannot be distinguished.
