# Week 3 – Section 4.8: Adverse Effects Validation Notes

## M027 – Amlodipine

Product: MIBRAL 5 mg  
Source PDF: amlodipine-RCP.pdf  
Section: 4.8 – Effets indésirables  
Source pages: 5, 6, 7

### Very common
- Edema.

### Common
- Somnolence.
- Dizziness.
- Headache.
- Visual disorders (including diplopia).
- Palpitations.
- Flushing.
- Dyspnea.
- Abdominal pain.
- Nausea.
- Dyspepsia.
- Bowel habit disorders (including diarrhea and constipation).
- Ankle edema.
- Muscle cramps.
- Fatigue.
- Asthenia.

### Uncommon
- Depression.
- Mood changes (including anxiety).
- Insomnia.
- Tremor.
- Dysgeusia.
- Syncope.
- Hypoesthesia.
- Paresthesia.
- Tinnitus.
- Arrhythmia (including bradycardia, ventricular tachycardia, atrial fibrillation).
- Hypotension.
- Cough.
- Rhinitis.
- Vomiting.
- Dry mouth.
- Alopecia.
- Purpura.
- Skin discoloration.
- Hyperhidrosis.
- Pruritus.
- Rash.
- Exanthem.
- Urticaria.
- Arthralgia.
- Myalgia.
- Back pain.
- Micturition disorders.
- Nocturia.
- Increased urinary frequency.
- Impotence.
- Gynecomastia.
- Chest pain.
- Pain.
- Malaise.
- Weight gain.
- Weight loss.

### Rare
- Confusion.

### Very rare
- Leucocytopenia.
- Thrombocytopenia.
- Allergic reaction.
- Hyperglycemia.
- Hypertonia.
- Peripheral neuropathy.
- Myocardial infarction.
- Vasculitis.
- Pancreatitis.
- Gastritis.
- Gingival hyperplasia.
- Hepatitis.
- Jaundice.
- Elevated liver enzymes.
- Angioedema.
- Erythema multiforme.
- Exfoliative dermatitis.
- Stevens-Johnson syndrome.
- Quincke's edema.
- Photosensitivity.

### Frequency not known
- Extrapyramidal disorder.
- Toxic epidermal necrolysis.

## M030 – Ramipril

Product: RAMIPRIL ALMUS 1,25 mg  
Source PDF: Résumé des Caractéristiques du Produit - RAMIPRIL ALMUS 1,25 mg.pdf  
Section: 4.8 – Effets indésirables  
Source page: 3

### Frequent
- Hyperkalemia.
- Headache.
- Dizziness.
- Hypotension.
- Syncope.
- Irritative cough.
- Bronchitis.
- Dyspnea.
- Digestive disorders.
- Nausea.
- Diarrhea.

### Uncommon
- Anorexia.
- Loss of appetite.
- Vertigo.
- Paresthesia.
- Dysgeusia.
- Flushing.
- Bronchospasm.
- Nasal congestion.
- Pancreatitis.
- Intestinal angioedema.

### Rare
- Tremor.
- Balance disorder.
- Vasculitis.
- Glossitis.

### Very rare / Unknown
- Hyponatremia.
- Cerebral ischemia.
- Stroke.
- Raynaud syndrome.
- Acute liver failure.

Note: The source combines "Very rare / Unknown" in one frequency column. Exact frequency cannot be distinguished.

## M031 – Enalapril

Product: ENALAPRIL ARROW 20 mg  
Source PDF: RCP- ENALAPRIL.pdf  
Section: 4.8 – Effets indésirables  
Source page: 2

### Very frequent or frequent
- Headache.
- Dizziness.
- Blurred vision.
- Hypotension.
- Cough.
- Nausea.
- Diarrhea.
- Asthenia.
- Hyperkalemia.
- Increased creatinine.

### Rare but serious
- Angioedema.
- Neutropenia / agranulocytosis.
- Hepatic or renal failure.

Note: The source groups "very frequent" and "frequent" together. Exact individual frequency is not specified.

Validation status: Manually checked against the original RCP, page 2.
